# Complaint Escalation Policy

## Escalation Triggers
1. SLA deadline exceeded without status update
2. Emergency complaints (urgency score = 10)
3. Root cause complaints (3+ recurring incidents) 
4. Suspected fraud cases
5. Low AI confidence requiring human review

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Department Supervisor
- Triggered when: SLA breach detected
- Notification method: Email + WhatsApp to supervisor
- Response required: Within 2 hours
- Action: Assign officer, provide status update

### Level 2: Municipal Manager  
- Triggered when: No response to Level 1 within 4 hours
- Notification method: Email + SMS to manager
- Response required: Within 1 hour
- Action: Direct intervention, resource reallocation

### Level 3: City Administrator
- Triggered when: Critical complaints unresolved after 24 hours
- Notification method: Formal report + direct call
- Response required: Immediate
- Action: Emergency protocols, media management

## Escalation Matrix by Urgency Score

### Score 8-10 (Critical)
- Level 1: Immediate notification
- Level 2: 2 hours if unresolved  
- Level 3: 4 hours if unresolved

### Score 6-7 (High)
- Level 1: SLA breach + 1 hour
- Level 2: SLA breach + 6 hours
- Level 3: SLA breach + 24 hours

### Score 4-5 (Medium)  
- Level 1: SLA breach + 2 hours
- Level 2: SLA breach + 12 hours
- Level 3: Not applicable

### Score 1-3 (Low)
- Level 1: SLA breach + 4 hours  
- Level 2: SLA breach + 24 hours
- Level 3: Not applicable

## Special Cases
- Anonymous complaints: Skip citizen notification, continue internal escalation
- Vulnerable group complaints: Accelerated escalation timeline
- Media attention cases: Direct escalation to Level 3
- Legal implications: Immediate legal department notification
