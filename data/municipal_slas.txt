# Municipal Service Level Agreements

## Roads Department SLA
- Pothole repairs: 72 hours from complaint registration
- Road damage assessment: 24 hours
- Major road reconstruction: 30 days planning period
- Emergency road hazards: 2 hours response time

## Water & Sanitation SLA  
- Water leak repairs: 24 hours
- Sewage blockage clearing: 12 hours
- Water quality complaints: 48 hours investigation
- Emergency water supply disruption: 4 hours restoration

## Solid Waste Management SLA
- Garbage collection complaints: 48 hours resolution
- Illegal dumping cleanup: 24 hours
- Recycling program queries: 7 days response
- Bulk waste removal: 5 business days

## Electrical Department SLA
- Streetlight repairs: 12 hours 
- Power line hazards: 2 hours emergency response
- New connection requests: 15 business days
- Electrical safety inspections: 7 days

## General Administration SLA
- General complaints: 7 days (168 hours)
- Document requests: 3 business days
- Policy clarifications: 5 business days
- Inter-department coordination: 48 hours
